03 / From browsing to a shortlist

How to use a
Litbuy spreadsheet.

A useful list preserves the details behind a choice. Keep original seller information beside your own notes so you can review each item before placing an order.

01.

Choose a category

Begin with shoes, outerwear, T-shirts or accessories. Separate categories so sizing and price comparisons stay relevant.

02.

Save the exact item

Record the original seller URL, product name, selected color and variant. A collection page alone may not identify the item later.

03.

Check measurements

Compare garment measurements with a piece you own. For footwear, check the seller’s chart and how foot length is measured.

04.

Review before ordering

Recheck stock, price, seller terms and platform support. Add delivery and other applicable costs before committing your budget.

04 / Better fields, clearer decisions

Make every row
earn its place.

Your Litbuy product spreadsheet should help answer three questions: what is the item, will it suit you, and what might it cost in total?

Keep estimates separate from confirmed figures. A listed item price is only one part of a shopping budget.

05 / Clear answers

Before you
start browsing.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Litbuy spreadsheet means a shopping shortlist that organizes product links, categories, sizes, prices and personal notes for comparison. It is not a guarantee of stock, quality or platform compatibility.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a browsing directory and an organization guide. Its category links open CAFANS; it does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet or a live Litbuy product feed.

Can every CAFANS link be used with Litbuy?

Compatibility is not verified here. Check the original seller URL, current platform support and any product restrictions directly before ordering.

What should I check before adding an item to my shortlist?

Check the original seller link, selected variant, measurements, material details, current price, availability and return conditions. For branded goods, verify provenance and authenticity independently.

How should I estimate my total shopping budget?

Record item prices separately from domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping and any applicable import costs. Keep estimates labeled until the relevant provider confirms them.
